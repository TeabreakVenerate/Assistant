# The Eisenhower Decision Matrix

The core priority engine used by the assistant to classify every brain dump and task list.

---

## The 4 Quadrants

### Q1: Urgent & Important (Crisis & Firefighting)
- **Characteristics:** Immediate deadline, severe consequence if ignored today.
- **Examples:** Tomorrow's exam, production server crash, urgent bill due today, major client emergency.
- **Action:** Execute immediately. Do not defer.

### Q2: Not Urgent but Important (Deep Work & Long-Term Growth)
- **Characteristics:** High leverage, builds the future, but has no artificial urgency pushing you today.
- **Examples:** Software development, exercise, study blocks, writing essays, building automated workflows.
- **Action:** Protect dedicated focus blocks in your calendar. If you do not schedule Q2, Q1 and Q3 will consume your life.

### Q3: Urgent but Not Important (Shallow Work & Distractions)
- **Characteristics:** Feels urgent to someone else, but contributes zero progress toward your primary goals.
- **Examples:** Most group chat notifications, trivial errand requests, urgent minor emails.
- **Action:** Batch into 30-minute low-energy windows, delegate, or defer.

### Q4: Neither Urgent nor Important (Waste & Escapism)
- **Characteristics:** Zero progress, high time drain, mindless avoidance of hard work.
- **Examples:** Infinite video scrolling, re-organizing folders that are already organized, debating on social media.
- **Action:** Flag ruthlessly and eliminate immediately.
